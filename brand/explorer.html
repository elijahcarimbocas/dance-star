Co-Brand Rules

Living next to the academy

Phoenix Bachata Academy keeps its red on its own surfaces. Dance Star never wears it, and this page will not render it either. The credit is one line of text.

Dance*Star
from the team behind Phoenix Bachata Academy

★Do credit the academy in text: "from the team behind Phoenix Bachata Academy."
★Do keep the star motif as the shared thread across both brands.
✕Never set Dance Star UI, buttons, or the wordmark in the academy's red.
✕Never mix two voice colors on one surface. One voice per screen, always.

Brand Architecture

Three models, one path

Each model maps to a growth stage. The visual rule holds in all three: Dance Star never wears the academy's red. Adjacency, never inheritance.

Now

Endorsed launch

Stage 01

Dance Star leads with its own name and palette. Phoenix Bachata Academy appears only as a text credit, never as color or logo on Dance Star surfaces. Borrows real trust with zero visual debt.

DANCE STAR
  "from the team behind
  Phoenix Bachata Academy"
Grow into

Sibling system

Stage 02

Dance Star becomes the platform brand and Phoenix Bachata Academy becomes its founding listed studio, with permanent "Founding Studio" placement. Separate palettes, shared star motif.

DANCE STAR (platform)
  ├ Phoenix Bachata Academy ★ founding
  ├ Studio B  ├ Studio C ...
End game

The flip

Stage 03

Dance Star is the parent. Studios adopt "a Dance Star studio" and keep their local colors, the academy's red included. The platform palette stays untouchable underneath them all.
