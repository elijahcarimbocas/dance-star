#1 / The Contrast Math

Measured, not eyeballed

Ratios computed with the WCAG 2.1 relative-luminance formula. The pink pairs are marginal and the system routes around that, so nobody has to re-derive it per page.

PairRatioWhat it means on the page
White on Fuchsia3.96:1Large text only. 24px regular or 19px bold and up. Covers every Anton headline; covers nothing smaller.
Ink on Fuchsia4.97:1Passes AA normal. The fix for every small label sitting on the pink field: set it in Ink, not white.
Deep Rose on White5.87:1Passes AA normal. The only small pink-colored text allowed on white or oat. Raw Fuchsia fails there.
Ink on White / Oat19.66 / 17.65Passes everything. The default for all reading text.
White on Fuchsia Dark8.09:1Passes AAA. The footer surface: pink family that holds small white text.
Muted on Oat6.09:1Passes AA normal. Captions, sub-labels, metadata.
Fuchsia on White, graphics3.96:1Legal for non-text. Borders, icon strokes, focus rings. As text below 24px: banned.
Success on Fuchsia1.35:1Fails everything. Semantic text never sits on the pink field. Put it in a white or ink card first.

#2 / Typography

Four faces, four jobs

All free on Google Fonts, all self-hostable. Hierarchy comes from size and case, never from adding families.

AntonDisplay. Uppercase always, line-height .9, one weight. Hero H1 caps at display-xl and holds at most two lines. Mass is not impact.
Pick an hour. Ask for it.
Fraunces ItalicKicker. The human line beside the shout. Hard cap 2rem, one line only, never more than 40% of the display size it sits beside.
Your rate, out in the open.
Public SansBody. 400 regular, 600 for emphasis, line-height 1.55 to 1.6. Built for plain-language legibility; it disappears in service of the facts.

#3 / Buttons + Pills

Pink is the field. Black is the switch.

The highest-emphasis action on any page is always Ink-filled. Three variants exist; a fourth is a bug.

1 · Primary

See open hours →

Solid Ink, white label. The only high-emphasis button. The arrow glyph rides primary buttons only, never ghosts.

2 · Ghost on fuchsia

I teach

Ink label, never white: 13.5px white on fuchsia fails AA. Hover fills white.

3 · Ghost on light

See what it looks like

Ink label, hairline border. Hover inverts to Ink fill.

Beginner friendly This week in Phoenix Pills default to Ink fill. Small pink labels on light surfaces render in Deep Rose.
★Labels are sentence case, first word capitalized, no trailing periods.
★Focus rings on everything: Ink ring on light surfaces, a white-then-ink double ring on the fuchsia field.
✕Fuchsia is never a button fill. A solid pink button reads like a cosmetics checkout.
✕No fourth variant. If a layout seems to need one, the layout is wrong.

#3 / The Banned List

Never, on any surface

✕The academy's red. Dance Star never wears Phoenix Bachata Academy red: not in UI, not in the wordmark, not in a button. Its hex stays out of this guide on purpose.
✕Fuchsia as a button fill. See the button rules. This is the fastest way to make the brand read cosmetic instead of athletic.
✕Gradients off fuchsia. No fade toward white or Blush. Pink is a flat field or a flat fill.
✕Blush as a dominant background. One capped wash per view, never stacked.
✕Pink-on-pink text of any kind.
✕White or raw Fuchsia text below 24px on the opposite color. Small labels on pink go Ink; small pink on light goes Deep Rose.
✕Script and cursive typefaces. The old site's flourish does not carry forward.
✕Hearts, flowers, lace, glitter motifs.
✕Soft-focus romantic-glow photography. Hard, direct light, the kind a dance floor actually has. Co-ed frames, shot for effort.
✕Em and en dashes, in copy and in markup. Hyphens only.
✕Star ratings before real reviews exist.
✕Exclamation points doing the enthusiasm's job.

The credit line. Phoenix Bachata Academy appears on Dance Star surfaces as text only: "from the team behind Phoenix Bachata Academy", set in Space Grotesk, Ink or Muted. Never its color, never its logo. Adjacency, not inheritance.

#4 / Voice

One person, talking to one person

A short claim, then the specific fact that backs it. A beat of reassurance right after the thing that might scare a beginner off. Real numbers over adjectives: $75 an hour, never "ask".

Approved, load-bearing

"Never danced? Perfect."Hero kicker
"Put your free hours where people can see them"Teach H1
"We went through 47 studios around Phoenix. Seven post a private rate anywhere a stranger could find it."Teach lede
"An hour happens once. Put it on the board."Closer

The kill list

  • ✕Rule-of-three rhythm in prose
  • ✕"Not X, it's Y" reversals and their cousins
  • ✕Not-only-but-also builds
  • ✕Grand abstract closers
  • ✕Uniform sentence length
  • ✕Passive voice where active exists
  • ✕Any claim the product cannot keep today

Say / never say

request an hourbook instantly, reserve
open hoursslots, sessions, availability windows
claim your pagesign up, onboard, get started
No payment moves on this page.any dollar the product touches

Banned words: unlock, seamless, elevate, empower, journey, effortless, transform, curated, and "community" as filler.
